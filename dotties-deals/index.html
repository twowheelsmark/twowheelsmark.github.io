Flower Deals in Los Angeles, CA

Great flowers should not have to wait for a special occasion. Our deals page gathers the arrangements and rose bundles we have on sale right now, from bright everyday bouquets to big rose arrangements.

A sale price never means a smaller effort. Every deal is designed by the same floral team, with the same fresh flowers, as the rest of our shop.

Why Los Angeles Shops Deals at Dottie's

Family Owned

We are a family flower shop, not a national order website. Our story started with a flower stand on a Hollywood street corner, and the same family still runs the shop.

Same Designers, Same Flowers

Sale or not, every arrangement is designed in our own Los Angeles shop with as many local and California grown flowers as we can find.

Satisfaction Guaranteed

Every order, deals included, is backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee, and our own drivers deliver it with an email confirmation when it arrives.

Flower Deals Delivered All Over Los Angeles

Our flagship shop at 18500 Sherman Way in Reseda delivers across most of north and central Los Angeles, the whole San Fernando Valley and parts of Ventura County, including Encino, Sherman Oaks, Studio City, Burbank, Pasadena, Downtown Los Angeles and Santa Monica.

Deals change often, so if you see something you love, order it while it is here. You can schedule delivery for a future date online or by phone.

Flower Deal Questions

Can sale flowers be delivered the same day? +

Yes. Deals go out with our same-day deliveries across our local area when you order early in the day. If it is getting late, call us at (818) 996-2603.

Are sale arrangements smaller or lower quality? +

No. Sale arrangements are designed by the same team with the same fresh flowers as the rest of our shop, and every order is backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.

How often do your deals change? +

Our deals change with the season and with what our growers have available, so check back often.

Can I use a coupon on a deal? +

Current offers and their terms are listed on our coupons page. Call us at (818) 996-2603 if you have a question about a specific offer.

Do you have a rewards program? +

Yes. Our Smileback Rewards loyalty program rewards repeat customers.

Can I pick up a sale arrangement? +

Yes. Choose pickup at checkout and collect your order at our Reseda shop at 18500 Sherman Way.
