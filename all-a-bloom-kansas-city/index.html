Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer same-day flower delivery in Kansas City, MO?

Yes. All A'Bloom offers same-day flower delivery across Kansas City, Missouri. The countdown at the top of this page shows how much time is left to order for delivery today, or call (816) 554-0069.

Where in Kansas City do you deliver?

We deliver to homes, apartments, offices, hospitals, funeral homes, churches and event venues throughout Kansas City, including downtown, Midtown, the Country Club Plaza, Brookside, the River Market and Crown Center. We also deliver across the metro, from North Kansas City and Gladstone to Independence, Raytown and Lee's Summit.

Can you deliver to Saint Luke's Hospital and other Kansas City hospitals?

Yes. Include the patient's full name and room number with your order. Some intensive care units do not allow fresh flowers, so check with the unit first or choose a plant or gift instead.

Do you make sympathy and funeral flowers?

Yes. We design sympathy arrangements, standing sprays, casket pieces and plants, and deliver them to funeral homes and churches across Kansas City. Our designers are glad to help you choose something meaningful within your budget.

Can I send flowers to Kansas City from another state?

Absolutely. Many of our customers order online from out of town for family and friends in Kansas City. Choose an arrangement, enter the Kansas City address and your card message, and we handle the design and delivery locally.

Can I visit one of your shops?

Yes. Our retail shop is at 5 SE 3rd Street in downtown Lee's Summit, and our floral design studio is at 1130 Swift in North Kansas City. Call (816) 554-0069 if you would like to pick up an order.
