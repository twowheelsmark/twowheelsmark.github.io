6Why Chicago Orders Fall Flowers From Bonnie Flowers

Autumn is when Chicago starts bringing color indoors. Our fall arrangements are put together in the shop on Irving Park Road with sunflowers, warm roses and harvest tones, then delivered across the city and suburbs.

Family Owned 80+ Years

THREE GENERATIONS ON IRVING PARK ROAD

Bonnie Flowers has been family owned for more than 80 years, and our team has arranged fall flowers for every kind of Chicago autumn, from early September sunflowers to Thanksgiving centerpieces. Every one is made by hand in our shop at 3400 W Irving Park Road.

Hand-Arranged

SUNFLOWERS, ROSES AND HARVEST COLOR

Our fall collection leans on sunflowers, orange and burgundy roses, chrysanthemums and berries. Designs like the Scents of Autumn Pitcher are arranged by hand in our shop and delivered by our team, so they arrive fresh instead of after a trip in a shipping box.

Keepsake Containers

A GIFT THAT STAYS ON THE TABLE

Several fall designs come in a ceramic pumpkin, a pitcher or a terra cotta pot, so the container keeps a spot on the counter after the flowers are done. They make easy hostess gifts for Friendsgiving or a Sunday dinner.

Fall Flower Delivery Across Chicago and the Suburbs

Fall in Chicago moves fast. One weekend it is apple picking weather and the next the heat is on, and flowers are one of the easiest ways to keep the warmth of the season inside. We send fall arrangements to homes, offices and dinner tables throughout Chicago and over 200 surrounding suburbs.

October brings Sweetest Day and Halloween, and November is all about Thanksgiving centerpieces and hostess gifts. Our shop is closed on Thanksgiving Day, so plan holiday deliveries for earlier in the week and order at least a day ahead.

Not sure which fall design fits? Call (773) 588-2040 and tell us who it is for. Our team will point you to something that suits the person, the room and the budget.

Family owned. Hand-arranged in Chicago. Delivered the same day when you order by 10:00 AM.

Frequently Asked Questions

What flowers are in your fall arrangements?

Our fall collection uses sunflowers, roses in orange, burgundy and peach, chrysanthemums, lilies, alstroemeria and seasonal berries and greens. Colors run from soft peach to deep burgundy, so there is something for both cheerful and elegant tastes.

Can I get same-day fall flower delivery in Chicago?

Yes. Order by 10:00 AM and we deliver the same day throughout Chicago and over 200 surrounding suburbs. If your timing is tight, call (773) 588-2040 and our team will tell you what is still possible today.

When should I order Thanksgiving flowers?

Our shop is closed on Thanksgiving Day, so schedule centerpieces and hostess flowers for delivery before the holiday. During holiday weeks, please place your order at least one day ahead.

Do you have fall centerpieces for the table?

Yes. Designs like the Sunflower Centerpiece and the Classic Cornucopia are made for the dining table, and the pumpkin and pitcher arrangements work well on a buffet or entry table.

Do any fall arrangements come in a keepsake container?

Several do. The Peachy Ceramic Pumpkin, Golden Pumpkin, Scents of Autumn Pitcher and Terra Cotta Sunflower Season all come in containers that can be reused long after the flowers.

What areas do you deliver fall flowers to?

We deliver throughout Chicago and over 200 surrounding suburbs from our shop at 3400 W Irving Park Road. A local delivery fee of $17.99 is added for each address. Call (773) 588-2040 if you are not sure whether an address is in range.
