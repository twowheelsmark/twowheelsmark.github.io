Plant Care Basics

A little care goes a long way. Share these simple tips with whoever receives your plant.

Peace Lilies

Bright, indirect light. Water when the top of the soil feels dry. Drooping leaves are its way of asking for a drink.

Dish Gardens

Several green plants in one container. Keep in bright, indirect light and water lightly when the soil feels dry.

Succulent Gardens

Plenty of bright light and very little water. Let the soil dry out completely between waterings.

Orchids

Bright, indirect light and a small drink about once a week. Never let the roots sit in standing water.

Keep every plant away from heating vents and cold drafts, especially in a Michigan winter. Questions? Call us at (734) 242-6565.

Plant Delivery FAQs

Do plants make a good gift?

Yes. Plants last far longer than cut flowers, which makes them a thoughtful choice for a housewarming, a thank you, a new job or a loved one recovering at home.

Can I get same-day plant delivery?

Yes. Monroe Florist offers same-day flower delivery in Monroe and nearby towns across Monroe County. Place your order before our daily cutoff, or call us at (734) 242-6565 if you are ordering later in the day and we will let you know what we can do.

What is a good sympathy plant?

A peace lily is a classic choice, a living memorial a family can keep for years. Browse our Sympathy Plants for more options for the home or the service.

How often should I water my plant?

For most plants, water when the top inch of soil feels dry. Succulents need much less, and no plant likes to sit in standing water, so empty the saucer after watering.

Can I send a plant to an office?

Yes. We hand-deliver to homes and businesses throughout Monroe County. Add any helpful details at checkout, such as a suite number or department. We do our best to honor specific delivery-time requests, but we cannot guarantee an exact time.

Can I add chocolates or wine?

Yes. Pair your plant with chocolates or a wine gift basket. For wine, the giver and recipient both must be of legal age, which is 21 in Michigan.

Need help choosing?

Call us at (734) 242-6565 and our team will help you pick the right plant.
