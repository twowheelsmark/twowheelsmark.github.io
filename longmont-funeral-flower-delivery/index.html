Timing, Etiquette and What to Know

  • Funeral deliveries are made at least one hour before the service starts. Tell us the service time when you order, or the visitation time if that comes first.
  • We need the name of the person who has died, plus the funeral home or church and the date and time of the service. If you have the obituary, everything we need is usually in it.
  • The casket spray comes from the immediate family. Friends and coworkers usually send a standing spray, a wreath, a service bouquet or flowers for the family's home.
  • Sign the card the way the family knows you. A company name means more with the department or the person the family knows.
  • Louisville Cemetery does not permit glass vases, and allows only flowers, vases, wreaths and flags within the headstone space. For a graveside service, sending the tribute to the funeral home is usually easiest.
  • Short on time? For same-day delivery we recommend ordering by noon. Same-day orders placed after noon and before 4:00 PM may carry an express delivery fee, so please call. On Saturdays the cutoff is 1:00 PM in Longmont and 11:00 AM for surrounding areas. For a service later today, call rather than ordering online.
  • If you cannot attend, flowers or a plant sent to the family's home are always appropriate, and an arrangement that arrives a few weeks later is often the one they remember.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are funeral flowers delivered?

At least one hour before the service start time. Tell us the time of the service when you order, and the visitation time if there is one.

What information do you need to send flowers to a funeral?

The full name of the person who has died, the funeral home or church, and the date and time of the service. Add your card message and how you would like it signed.

Which funeral homes do you deliver to?

All of them in our area, including Ahlberg Funeral Chapel, Howe Mortuary & Crematory and Carroll-Lewellen in Longmont, Crist Mortuary and Greenwood & Myers in Boulder, and funeral homes in Lafayette and Frederick. If yours is not listed, call (303) 776-2804.

Who sends the casket spray?

Traditionally the immediate family. Everyone else sends a standing spray, a wreath, a service bouquet or flowers to the family's home.

Can I order funeral flowers for the same day?

Same-day delivery is often available. For same-day delivery we recommend ordering by noon. Same-day orders placed after noon and before 4:00 PM may carry an express delivery fee, so please call. On Saturdays the cutoff is 1:00 PM in Longmont and 11:00 AM for surrounding areas.

Can I order from out of state?

Yes. Many of our sympathy orders come from out of state. Order online or call (303) 776-2804, and we will coordinate with the funeral home and confirm when the flowers are delivered.
