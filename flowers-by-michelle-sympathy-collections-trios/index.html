Sympathy Flower Trios for Services in Las Vegas and Henderson

When a family is planning a service, matching flowers bring a sense of peace and order to the room. Our sympathy trios are designed by hand at our Las Vegas and Henderson shops as a coordinated set, so every piece shares the same flowers, colors and feeling.

Each trio is usually chosen by close family members and delivered to the funeral home or church before the service begins. If you would like different colors, a favorite flower or a personal touch, call either shop and our designers will create a set that reflects your loved one.

What Is in a Sympathy Trio?

A trio brings together the three main pieces for a service. You can also choose each piece on its own.

Casket Spray

The centerpiece of the service, placed on top of the casket. See all Casket Sprays.

Standing Spray

A tall arrangement on an easel, displayed beside the casket or near a photo. See Wreaths and Sprays.

Urn Arrangement

Flowers designed to surround or frame an urn at a memorial. See Cremation flowers.

Sending flowers as a friend rather than family? See Floral Arrangements for the Service, Flowers for the Home or Plants for Sympathy.

Sympathy Trio FAQs

Who usually orders a sympathy trio?

Trios are most often chosen by close family members, since the casket spray and standing spray are usually the main pieces at the service. Friends and extended family often send a single arrangement or a plant.

Can you change the colors or flowers in a trio?

Yes. We specialize in custom floral designs. Call either shop and our designers will adjust the colors, flowers or add a personal touch that reflects your loved one.

Can you deliver to a funeral home or church?

Yes. We deliver to funeral homes and churches across Las Vegas and Henderson. Include the name of the deceased, the funeral home or church, and the date and time of the service so the flowers arrive before it begins.

How soon should I order a sympathy trio?

As soon as the service details are set. If the service is soon, call us at (702) 255-0209 so we can confirm timing before you order. We do not deliver on Sundays.

Can I order the pieces separately?

Yes. You can choose a casket spray, a standing spray or wreath or a cremation arrangement on its own.

Which areas do you deliver to?

Our shops deliver to Las Vegas, Henderson, North Las Vegas, Summerlin, Green Valley, The Lakes, Sun City Summerlin, Spanish Trail, Lake Las Vegas and Southern Highlands. If you don't see your area, call us at (702) 255-0209.

We're here to help

Call our designers in Las Vegas at (702) 255-0209 or Henderson at (702) 445-6000 and we'll gently guide you through the choices.
