    Funeral Flower Delivery

    1Funeral Flower Delivery in Gresham and the Portland Area

    Nancy's Floral designs casket sprays, standing sprays, wreaths and sympathy arrangements for funeral homes, churches and cemeteries across Gresham, Portland and Clackamas County. Every tribute is made by hand in our Gresham shop and delivered by our own team, timed to arrive before the family and guests do.

    Funeral flowers run on someone else's schedule. Tell us the name of the person who has passed and where the service is being held, and we will work out the service time and plan the delivery around it. If you are unsure what to send, call and a designer will talk it through with you.

    Need flowers for a service?

    Call and a designer will help you choose, confirm the service details and schedule the delivery. For tight timing, a phone call is always the fastest way to get flowers there.

    How Funeral Delivery Works

    1

    Tell us the name and the place

    The full name of the person who has passed, the funeral home, church or cemetery, and the service date and time if you know them.

    2

    We confirm the details

    We verify service and viewing times where we can. For a church or hall, we ask when someone will be there to receive the flowers.

    3

    We deliver before the service

    Your tribute arrives ahead of the family and guests, placed and ready. At cemeteries we follow the operator's rules so nothing is removed early.

    Ordering Times, Etiquette and Cemetery Rules

    • Order early when you can. Our recommended minimums: for a Monday morning service, order by noon Saturday. For a Tuesday through Saturday morning service, order by 3:00 PM the day before. For an afternoon service, allow at least four hours. Casket sprays, standing sprays and custom tributes may need more time. We are closed Sundays.
    • Services at churches and halls. Unlike a funeral home, many churches, halls and Kingdom Halls are not staffed all day, and Saturday services are often locked until shortly before they start. Tell us when someone will be there to receive the flowers, and a name and mobile number if you have one.
    • Willamette National Cemetery handles service flowers differently. Arrangements go to the assigned committal shelter about 15 to 30 minutes before the service, because shelters are used for several services a day. The cemetery office opens at 9:00 AM, so early services need extra planning. Fresh flowers may be placed on graves at any time. Artificial flowers are allowed only from November 1 to March 1 and around Memorial Day. Glass vases, pots, planters and balloons are removed.
    • Gethsemani and Mount Calvary Catholic Cemeteries welcome fresh flowers at outdoor graves year round. Artificial, dyed or preserved flowers, glass containers and wreaths are not allowed except on holidays the cemetery designates, and potted plants over six inches are not allowed outside those holidays. Glass is prohibited at all times.
    • Metro's pioneer cemeteries, including Gresham Pioneer, Pleasant Home, Douglass, Mountain View Corbett, Powell Grove, Columbia Pioneer and Lone Fir, do not allow vases. Funeral flowers and easels must be removed within three weeks, and artificial flowers are not permitted from March 1 to November 15 except around Memorial Day.
    • Mountain View Cemetery in Oregon City removes funeral pieces once they wilt, and families who want to keep them must collect them within 48 hours of the burial. No glass, and no artificial flowers from March 1 to October 31.
    • Estacada Cemetery District cemeteries allow no cans or glass vases, and no artificial flowers or permanent containers during mowing season, March 1 to November 15.
    • River View Cemetery will place flowers delivered to its office on the grave, crypt or niche. For any grave placement, send us the cemetery, full name, and section, lot or plot if you have it.
    • Check the obituary. If the family asks for donations in lieu of flowers, a plant or a small arrangement sent to their home is still a kind gesture, and often arrives when the house has gone quiet.

    Cemetery rules above are quoted from each operator's published policy, checked September 2026: VA National Cemetery Administration (Willamette National Cemetery), Catholic Cemeteries of the Archdiocese of Portland, Metro Parks and Nature cemetery visiting rules, City of Oregon City Mountain View Cemetery rules, and Estacada Cemetery Maintenance District rules. Ordering times and the Willamette National process come from Nancy's Floral's own delivery policy. Rules change, so we confirm when a delivery is going graveside.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Do you deliver funeral flowers to funeral homes in Gresham and Portland?

    Yes. We deliver to funeral homes, churches and cemeteries across Gresham, Portland and Clackamas County, including Bateman Carroll and Gresham Memorial Chapel in Gresham, Lincoln Memorial Park, Gethsemani and Sunnyside in Happy Valley, Sandy Funeral Home and Estacada Funeral Chapel. The full list is above.

    How far ahead should I order funeral flowers?

    For a Monday morning service, order by noon Saturday. For Tuesday through Saturday morning services, order by 3:00 PM the day before. Afternoon services need at least four hours. Larger tributes like casket and standing sprays may need more notice, so call (503) 661-0911 if the service is soon.

    What information do you need for a funeral delivery?

    The full name of the person who has passed, the service location, the service date, and the viewing and service times if you know them. For a church or hall, tell us when someone will be there to accept the flowers and give us a contact name and mobile number if you can.

    Can you deliver flowers to Willamette National Cemetery?

    Yes. For a committal service we deliver to the assigned shelter shortly before the service, usually 15 to 30 minutes ahead, and we may confirm the time and shelter with the cemetery that morning. Please order in advance, especially for early morning services.

    Do you place flowers on graves?

    Yes. We offer grave placement at cemeteries throughout the Portland area. Send us the cemetery name, the full name of the person, and any section, lot, row or plot number you have. We follow each cemetery's rules on containers and artificial flowers.

    Do you deliver funeral flowers on Sunday?

    No, Nancy's Floral is normally closed on Sundays. For a Monday morning service, please order by noon on Saturday so we can have the flowers there on time.
