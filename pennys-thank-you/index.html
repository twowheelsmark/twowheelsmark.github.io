Thank You Flowers Delivered Across the Philadelphia Region

A thank you note gets read once. Thank you flowers sit on the counter all week and remind someone every time they walk by that what they did mattered. Every thank you arrangement from Penny's by Plaza Flowers is designed by hand in our Norristown design studio and delivered by our own drivers to homes, offices and schools throughout Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs, seven days a week.

Thank a host after a great dinner, a teacher at the end of the year, or the nurse who made a hospital stay easier. For clients and business partners, see our corporate gifts.

Why Customers Say Thanks with Penny's

Farm-Direct Flowers

We buy many of our flowers directly from growers in Ecuador, Colombia, Holland and California. Fresher flowers last longer, so your thanks stays on display.

Award-Winning Designers

Our design team has been named Pennsylvania Florist of the Year and ranked among the top florists by Teleflora and FTD. Every arrangement is made by hand in our design studio.

Family Owned Since 1937

Penny's opened in Glenside in 1937, and the Drummond family knows how much a simple thank you can mean.

Thank You Flower FAQs

Can I get same-day delivery on thank you flowers?

Yes. We deliver the same day, seven days a week, across Philadelphia and the surrounding suburbs. Order early in the day, or call us at (610) 272-2233 to confirm timing.

What flowers say thank you?

Pink roses stand for gratitude, and bright mixed bouquets with gerbera daisies, sunflowers and lilies feel warm and cheerful. A blooming plant is a lovely thank you that lasts.

Can you deliver to an office or a school?

Yes. Add the company or school name, the room or suite, and any notes for the front desk at checkout so your flowers reach the right person.

What should I write on a thank you card?

Be specific. "Thank you for making our stay so easy" or "Thank you for everything you did for Mom" means more than a general thanks.

Can I add chocolates or a gift?

Yes. You can add chocolates, balloons and plush animals to most arrangements.

How will I know they received the flowers?

We send you an email as soon as your flowers are delivered.

Need help choosing?

Call our designers at (610) 272-2233 and we'll help you say thank you in style.
