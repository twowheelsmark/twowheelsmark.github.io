FAQs

What Towns Do You Deliver Flowers To?

We deliver from our shop in Bedford Village to Bedford, Bedford Hills, Bedford Corners, Katonah, Pound Ridge, Mt Kisco, Armonk, Chappaqua, Cross River, South Salem, Waccabuc, Goldens Bridge, North Salem, Somers and Purdys in New York, and to Greenwich, Cos Cob, Stamford, New Canaan and Ridgefield in Connecticut. See our delivery areas page for details, or call 914-234-7180 if you do not see your town.

Do You Offer Same-Day Flower Delivery?

Yes. Order online by 3pm Monday through Friday, or by noon on Saturday, for same-day flower delivery in Bedford and the surrounding towns. Past the cutoff? Call us at 914-234-7180 and we will check what we can do.

What if the Recipient Isn’t Home When You Deliver the Flowers?

Don’t worry – we call before delivery to confirm the recipient will be home.

How Will My Flowers Arrive?

All flower arrangements are fresh-cut and made the same day of delivery!

Do You Ship Flowers or Gifts?

Our flower arrangements and gifts are available for in-store pick up or local delivery. You are welcome to shop online, or stop by our store in person to make your selections. We would love to help you find the perfect gift! 

How Far in Advance Do I Need To Order My Wedding Flowers?

To ensure that we can fulfill your order according to your exact specifications, we recommend that you book your wedding flower order 3 to 6 months in advance.

Is a Consultation Required?

Yes! Our consultation process first involves a phone call to make sure the budget and vision are in alignment. Once we sort out your design ideas and preferred price(s), our florists will schedule a consultation that entails a full review of your detailed proposal. We do this to ensure all your needs and expectations are met.

What Is Your Pricing?

Our average wedding order is between $8,000-$10,000. We provide an “à la carte” proposal, breaking out every element and detail of the wedding.

What if I Do Not Know Exactly What Type of Flowers I Want for My Wedding?

Our designers can help! We’ll create our floral arrangements based on your color palette and desired style or aesthetic. We also consider the venue.

What if the Flowers I Want Are Not in Season?

If the flowers you want are not in season, we’ll propose an alternative.

Do You Have a Wedding Flower Gallery?

Yes, we do have a wedding flower gallery! Each of the ceremonies represented in our gallery represents a unique reflection of every couple’s personality, design aesthetic, and wedding style.

What Special Events Do You Provide Flower Services To?

We provide flowers for communions, Bar/Bat Mitzvahs, bridal showers, baby showers, birthdays, private parties, galas, horse shows, weddings, corporate events, and holiday parties.

Can I See Samples of My Special Event Flowers?

Yes! Once you’ve signed with us for an event and as long as you meet the qualifying minimum, we provide sample arrangements based on your specific proposal.

Do You Offer Specific Packages or Is Everything Customized?

All our flower packages are customized to your exact specifications and ideas.

How Do You Make Your Flowers Last Longer?

Our florists start by sourcing all premium flowers from trusted vendors we have developed long-standing relationships with. From there we follow a rigorous conditioning process for the flowers based on each individual flower type and care requirements. Some flowers need the use of rapid hydration products, others require submergence into boiling water.

How Do You Keep Flower Arrangements Fresh?

Re-cut the tips of the stems and change the water daily. Follow our floral expert’s tips for caring for fresh flowers for more information!

What Are the Benefits of a Flower Subscription?

There are many benefits to a flower subscription from Bedford Village Flower Shoppe. For one, a subscription to our flower delivery services ensures you’ll always have fresh-cut, beautiful floral arrangements in your home. Sign up for a flower subscription and let us do the rest!

Where Do Your Flowers Come From?

We source our flowers from the highest quality growers. Sometimes they come from Italy or Holland, and other times they come from a farm in upstate NY. Wherever they are the freshest, in-season picks!

Two Wheels Marketing · October 2026

Homepage Recommendations

Changes to help Bedford Village Flower Shoppe rank for local searches like "florist Bedford NY" and "flower delivery Katonah." Numbered items are highlighted on the page.

Everything else stays as it is today: the design, photos, product grid, weddings and Lauren sections. All changes can be made in the current WordPress setup (Elementor, Yoast SEO and the Structured Data plugin already installed), with no new plugins or redesign.

Why

This small line is the page's main heading (H1), one of the strongest on-page signals. Today it only repeats the shop name, which already ranks first. The look stays exactly the same, and the shop name still leads the logo, menu and hero.
