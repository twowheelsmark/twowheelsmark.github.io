    FAQs

    Orders can be placed 24/7 on our ecommerce site: nancysfloral.com or we can be reached by phone during normal business hours at 503-661-0911

    Nancy's Floral accepts Cash, check, credit card, debit card, PayPal, PayPal Pay Later, Amazon Pay, Google Pay, Apple Pay & Venmo as forms of payment.

    Same-day local and national delivery is available when the order is placed before the designated cut-off times per area.

    Orders can be scheduled for same-day delivery, when placed before the designated cut-off times, and for specific days or months into the future at the time of order.

    We will normally leave the item at the door and call the number provided at time of delivery if necessary. The driver will also take a picture of the item left at the door that will appear digitally in the delivery confirmation email. In rare cases we may also leave a note on the recipient's door asking the recipient to call us and arrange a convenient time for re-delivery. We employ professional delivery personnel who will find the best delivery choice for your special gift. Many times the best option is to send the delivery to a workplace. We will honor requests to “leave at door if not home,” however, if the delivery driver feels that the location is not safe, due to bad weather, extreme heat or cold, or theft potential, then the delivery may need to be rescheduled.

    We offer discounts for bulk flowers and corporate clients. Nancy's Floral serves many DIY wedding customers with superior quality and excellent pricing for bulk loose flowers. Corporate clients can achieve preferred pricing for large events for flowers and delivery on a case-by-case basis.

    A personalized message can be added to any order, whether by phone or our e-commerce site. Our website also offers message ideas for all occasions in the card message section for help when words or expressions are difficult to formulate.

    To enhance your gift, we offer many options; chocolate truffles, plush animals, mylar balloons for all occasions, greeting cards, and even succulent chocolate dipped strawberries during select holidays.

    All cut floral arrangements are made with a floral preservative in the water that promotes longevity and blooming of the flowers contained within. Container arrangements will require that water is added daily to achieve the longest bouquet life. It is very important that the vase or container stays full. Also, flowers should never be put in direct sun or drafty areas, as these will dehydrate and degrade the bouquet prematurely.

    Most arrangements are made and delivered the same day. Nancy's Floral carries a diverse variety of fresh flowers that arrive from local growers and exotic locations daily. Standing orders from Hawaii, Holland, North & South America and trusted local farms assure the broadest selection and the freshest products available.

    Keep your flowers in a cool spot (65 to 72 degrees F). As pretty as they may look, don't keep them in direct sunlight. Also, avoid placing your flowers where heating or cooling vents can blow air on them - any areas directly under ceiling fans, or on top of appliances that may give off heat (older televisions, radiators, etc.), as this will cause your flowers to dehydrate much faster. Also, make sure to water your flowers daily, keeping vases and containers full.

    Depending on the season, our seasonal offerings highlight many popular garden flowers not available other times of the year. The Summer seasonals, for example, might contain; Dahlias, Zinnias, Crocosmia, MontBretia, Astilbe, Cosmos, Amaranthus and more.

    Most of our flowers are eco-friendly or organic. The majority of our flowers come from world-renowned growers in Ecuador and Columbia where innovative agricultural practices increase productivity while ensuring environmental sustainability. We also source flowers from local growers that use sustainable garden practices and are completely chemical-free.

    Flowers have long been cherished as a thoughtful and beautiful gift for various occasions, each with its own symbolic meaning. For birthdays, bright and cheerful flowers like gerbera daisies, sunflowers, and lilies are popular choices as they represent joy and celebration. Roses, particularly in red or pink, are ideal for anniversaries, symbolizing love, passion, and enduring commitment. For a more personalized touch, consider selecting flowers that align with the recipient's birth month or favorite colors. Orchids and tulips also make elegant choices for anniversaries, symbolizing love, beauty, and a deep connection. Whether celebrating a special milestone or expressing love and appreciation, the right flowers can convey the perfect sentiment for any occasion.

    Nancy's Floral specializes in flowers for weddings and special events. We strive to make your dreams a reality. Since no two weddings are alike, we do not have pre-set wedding packages, but instead make each wedding as unique as the couple ordering. We design for events of all styles and guarantee the highest quality blooms at the best value.

    Nancy's Floral can create custom arrangements for your next corporate event from the simplest to the most complex design. Centerpieces, buffet table flowers, and podium arrangements can be customized to meet the theme, colors, and season of the event.
