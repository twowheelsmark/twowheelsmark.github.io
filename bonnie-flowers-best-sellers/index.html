Frequently Asked Questions

What makes an arrangement a best seller at Bonnie Flowers?

These are the designs Chicago customers order from us most often. They work for birthdays, thank yous, anniversaries and just because, and our designers arrange every one by hand in our shop at 3400 W Irving Park Road.

Can I get same-day delivery on best sellers?

Yes. Order by 10:00 AM and we deliver the same day throughout Chicago and over 200 surrounding suburbs. If your timing is tight, call (773) 588-2040 and we will tell you what is still possible today.

Which best seller should I choose if I do not know their taste?

A garden style bouquet in soft colors, like the Parisian Market Bouquet, suits almost anyone. If you would rather leave it to us, Bonnie's Exclusive Design lets our designers create something from the freshest flowers in the shop that day.

Can I add a card message or ask for a change?

Every order includes a card message you add at checkout. If you are hoping for a certain color or flower, call (773) 588-2040 before you order and our designers will tell you what they can do.

How much is delivery?

A local delivery fee of $17.99 is added for each delivery address. For addresses outside our local delivery area the fee may vary, so call us if you are not sure.

Do you deliver on Sundays?

Our shop is closed on Sundays, but we do make Sunday deliveries for funerals, weddings, showers and some other special occasions. Call (773) 588-2040 if you need a Sunday delivery. Other orders requested for Sunday are delivered the next business day.
