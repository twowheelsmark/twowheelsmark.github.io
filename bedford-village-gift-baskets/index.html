Each of our gift baskets is curated by hand and brimming with goodies chosen to delight. Whether you are celebrating a special occasion, expressing gratitude, or simply showing someone you care, a basket from our shop makes the gesture memorable.

Pair a basket with one of our floral arrangements for a bigger celebration, or stop by our boutique for candles, glassware, vases and hostess gifts.

The same eye for color, balance and texture that goes into our flowers goes into every basket we put together.

We take pride in selecting high-quality products for our luxury gift baskets, from gourmet treats to tea and preserves.

Gift Basket FAQs

Do you offer gift basket delivery?

Yes. We deliver gift baskets throughout Westchester, NY and Fairfield County, CT. See all our delivery locations.

What is the difference between Classic, Premium and Deluxe?

Classic baskets are stocked with all the essentials and carefully curated items. Premium baskets add a more lavish assortment of goodies. Deluxe baskets are our finest, designed with all the little details in mind for a grand statement.

Which gift basket should I choose?

Choose the Gourmet Basket for the food lover, Home Sweet Home Office for someone who works from home, Tea-Time Treats for a host or hostess, and the DIY Floral Gift Basket for anyone who loves to arrange flowers.

How do you keep the items fresh?

Maintaining the freshness and quality of every item is a top priority. We carefully package each item to preserve its freshness during delivery.

Can I pick up a gift basket at the shop?

Yes. All orders are available for pickup at our shop at 634 Old Post Road in Bedford or for local delivery. We do not currently ship our products.
