Luxury Floral Design in Brookline

Winston Flowers has long been part of life’s most meaningful moments across Brookline and Greater Boston. Founded on Newbury Street in Boston in 1944, our designers have created exceptional floral arrangements for everything from intimate gatherings at home to elegant celebrations throughout the region for more than 80 years.

Today, we offer same-day flower delivery to every corner of Brookline, from the shops along Harvard Street in Coolidge Corner to Brookline Village, Washington Square and the homes around the Brookline Reservoir. Our nearest shop is at 27 Boylston Street in Chestnut Hill. Whether you’re sending a birthday bouquet to a home near the Coolidge Corner Theatre or a centerpiece for a dinner party in Washington Square, Winston Flowers delivers with the care, artistry, and exceptional service that have defined us for decades.

Flowers for Every Brookline Occasion

From Shabbat dinners and holiday tables to the hardest goodbyes, we deliver flowers to the places that matter most to Brookline families.

Celebrations & Events

From b’nai mitzvahs and engagement parties to milestone birthdays, our designers create centerpieces and event flowers for celebrations of every size. We deliver to Brookline venues such as YJP Event Space on Chapel Street, the Larz Anderson Auto Museum and the Brookline Arts Center.

Plan your event flowers

Synagogues & Churches

We deliver flowers for holidays, b’nai mitzvahs, weddings and services at Brookline’s houses of worship, including Temple Sinai, Temple Ohabei Shalom, Congregation Kehillath Israel, Temple Beth Zion, Young Israel of Brookline, Maimonides Minyan, United Parish, St. Mary of the Assumption, All Saints Parish, the Episcopal Church of Our Saviour and St. Paul’s Church. Many congregations have their own guidelines for sanctuary flowers, so it’s worth checking with the office before you order.

Sympathy & Funeral Flowers

We deliver sympathy arrangements to Brookline funeral homes, including Bell-O’Dea Funeral Home, Stanetsky Memorial Chapel and Levine Chapels, and to the family’s home. In Jewish tradition, flowers are not customary at the funeral or during shiva, so many people send a gourmet gift box to the family instead.

Why choose Winston Flowers

80+ years of Greater Boston heritage

Founded in 1944 on Newbury Street, Winston Flowers is a third-generation, family-owned business led by David Winston. For more than eight decades, we've been part of Greater Boston’s story—trusted for extraordinary flowers, refined design, and thoughtful service.

World-class floral designers

Our designers push the boundaries of traditional floral design. Timeless techniques meet fresh perspectives, with collections refreshed nine times per year to reflect the finest flowers of the season.

Same-day delivery & concierge service

We source exceptional flowers from trusted growers around the world, with many varieties rarely seen elsewhere. With same-day delivery throughout Greater Boston and 5-star concierge care, every arrangement arrives looking its best.

Luxury gift presentation

Every Winston arrangement arrives in our signature packaging—an unmistakable finishing touch. From careful wrapping to the final reveal, we think the experience should be just as beautiful as the flowers themselves.

Award-winning excellence 

Winston Flowers has been recognized as one of the nation's most highly regarded florists as well as one of Boston's 'Best Of.' It's a reflection of the standards we hold, and the trust generations of clients place in us.
