Many arrive in keepsake containers like ceramic pumpkins, mosaic vases and pitchers, so the gift keeps going after the flowers are done. Send a bouquet to a friend in Encinitas, set a centerpiece on your own table in Scripps Ranch, or plan ahead with our Thanksgiving designs.

Fall Flower Questions

10
Which flowers do you use in fall arrangements?

Sunflowers are the star of the season, joined by roses, lilies, alstroemeria, mums, carnations and gerbera daisies. Some designs add eucalyptus and hydrangea, and a few are set in ceramic pumpkin containers you can keep and fill again every year.

Do you have flowers for the Thanksgiving table?

Yes. Our Thanksgiving collection has designs made for the holiday table and for thanking a host. Thanksgiving week is one of our busiest, so order a few days ahead if you can.

Can I get fall flowers delivered today?

Yes. Same-day delivery is available, and the earlier you order, the better. Our schedule can fill up on busy days, so if it needs to arrive today, order in the morning or call us at 858.484.7700.

How do I keep a fall bouquet fresh longer?

Top up the water every day, since sunflowers drink a lot. Keep the arrangement away from heaters, direct sun and the fruit bowl, and give the stems of a hand-tied bouquet a fresh trim every few days.

Where do you deliver fall flowers?

We deliver throughout North County San Diego, as far south as downtown, east to Ramona and Tierrasanta, and west to the beach communities. The delivery charge depends on the recipient’s zip code.

Can I pick up a fall arrangement at the shop?

Yes. Choose pick up when you order online and pick the time you want it ready, or stop by our shop at 13289 Black Mountain Road in the Vons center in Rancho Peñasquitos.
