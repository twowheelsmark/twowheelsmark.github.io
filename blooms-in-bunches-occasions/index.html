Flower Delivery FAQs

Can I get same-day flower delivery?

Yes. Blooms in Bunches offers same-day flower delivery across Merrick and Nassau County when you order by our daily cutoff. To confirm timing for a specific address, call us at (516) 481-1277.

What flowers should I send for an anniversary?

Red roses convey deep, lasting love, peonies symbolize a happy marriage, orchids represent love that keeps growing, and tulips stand for perfect love. Shop our Anniversary collection.

What flowers are appropriate for sympathy?

Lilies, white roses, hydrangeas, orchids and gladioli are all traditional sympathy flowers. See our Sympathy collections, or call (516) 481-1277 for help choosing.

Do you deliver to hospitals and funeral homes?

Yes. We deliver to hospitals, rehabilitation centers and funeral homes across Nassau County every day. See our Hospital Flower Delivery and Funeral Flower Delivery pages.

Do you do wedding and event flowers?

Yes. We design bridal bouquets, ceremony flowers and centerpieces for weddings, and flowers for sweet 16s, bar and bat mitzvahs and corporate events. Start on our Weddings and Events page.

How early should I order for a holiday?

We recommend placing orders at least 24 hours before any major holiday. Our shop is closed on Christmas Day and New Year's Day.

Need help choosing?

Call our designers at (516) 481-1277 and we'll help you find the right flowers for any occasion.
