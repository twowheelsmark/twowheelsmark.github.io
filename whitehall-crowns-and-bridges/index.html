Crowns and bridgesin Whitehall, OH.

A cracked, worn, or badly decayed tooth needs more than a filling to stay strong, and a missing tooth leaves a gap that affects how you chew. At Whitehall Family Dental, crowns protect damaged teeth and bridges replace missing ones, so you can eat and smile comfortably again.

Request an Appointment

When you need a crown

A crown is a cap that covers the whole tooth. We recommend one when a tooth is cracked, worn down, weakened by a large filling, or has had a root canal. The crown holds the tooth together and restores its shape so you can bite down without worry.

Bridges for missing teeth

A bridge fills the space left by a missing tooth. A replacement tooth is attached to crowns on the teeth on either side of the gap. It keeps your other teeth from drifting, helps you chew evenly, and fills in your smile.

What to expect

We numb the tooth, shape it, and take an impression for your crown or bridge. You will wear a temporary while the final one is made, then come back to have it fitted and adjusted so your bite feels right.

Making it last

Brush twice a day, floss around your crown or under your bridge, and keep up with your checkups. Regular visits let us catch decay around the edges early, which is the most common reason a crown or bridge needs to be replaced.

Questionsabout crowns and bridges.

What is a dental crown?

A custom cap that covers a damaged tooth to restore its shape, strength, and function.

What is a dental bridge?

A fixed replacement for one or more missing teeth, anchored to crowns on the neighboring teeth.

Does getting a crown hurt?

The tooth is numbed first, so you should not feel pain during treatment. Some tenderness afterward is normal for a few days.

How many visits does a crown take?

Usually two: one to prepare the tooth and place a temporary, and one to fit the final crown.

How long do crowns last?

With good care and regular checkups, crowns and bridges can last many years.

Do you accept Medicaid?

Yes. We accept Ohio Medicaid plans, including AmeriHealth Caritas, Buckeye Health Plan, Humana Healthy Horizons, Molina, and UnitedHealthcare Community Plan. We will check what your plan covers before we begin.
