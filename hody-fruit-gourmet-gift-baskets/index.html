Gift Baskets Delivered Across Nashville and Middle Tennessee

Sometimes the best gift is one everyone can share. Our fruit, snack, and gourmet gift baskets are a favorite for office thank yous, new parents, get well wishes, and families who are grieving, and our own drivers deliver them to homes and offices throughout Nashville, Franklin, Brentwood, and the surrounding communities.

Want flowers too? Choose a design that brings fruit and flowers together, or call the shop and we will help you put the right gift together.

Gift Basket Delivery FAQs

What is in your gift baskets?

Our baskets range from fresh fruit and snacks to gourmet treats, and some pair fruit with fresh flowers. Each listing shows what is included, and if you have something special in mind, call (615) 876-7181.

Can I get same-day delivery on a gift basket?

Yes. Hody's makes same-day deliveries in Nashville, Franklin, Brentwood, Cool Springs, Antioch, Bellevue, Goodlettsville, Hermitage, Hendersonville, Joelton, and Madison. Order early in the day for the best selection, or call us at (615) 876-7181 for anything time-sensitive.

Are gift baskets a good sympathy gift?

Yes. A fruit or snack basket sent to the family home is a thoughtful, practical way to help while relatives and friends gather. Browse our sympathy flowers too.

Can you deliver gift baskets to an office?

Yes. Our drivers deliver to homes, offices, and other businesses throughout our service area. Include the business name, the recipient, and any contact details in your order so your flowers reach the right person.

Do you offer house accounts for businesses?

Yes. We offer house accounts for businesses, churches, and civic organizations across Nashville and Middle Tennessee. Call (615) 876-7181 to set one up.

Can I send a gift basket outside the Nashville area?

Yes. For deliveries outside our area, we send your order through a network of preferred Teleflora florists we have built over more than 70 years, and every wired order is backed by our satisfaction guarantee.

Need Help Choosing?

Call our Nashville shop at (615) 876-7181 or our Cool Springs shop at (615) 373-9887 and we will help you choose the right gift basket.
