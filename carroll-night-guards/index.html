Night guardsin Bexley, OH.

Grinding or clenching your teeth at night can wear down enamel, crack teeth, and leave you with a sore jaw and morning headaches. A custom night guard from Carroll Family Dental in Bexley takes the force off your teeth while you sleep. We make each guard from a digital scan, so it fits snugly and comfortably.

Schedule a Guard Fitting

Signs you might grind

Many people grind in their sleep without knowing it. Common clues include a sore or stiff jaw when you wake up, frequent headaches, sensitive teeth, and teeth that look worn, flat, or chipped. A partner may even hear it at night. We also look for wear patterns at every checkup.

How a guard protects

A night guard is a thin barrier between your upper and lower teeth. Instead of tooth grinding on tooth, the guard absorbs the pressure, which protects your enamel and dental work and gives tired jaw muscles a break. Guards are also often recommended to ease TMJ symptoms.

Custom fit, not boil and bite

Store bought guards are bulky and often fit loosely, so many people stop wearing them. We take a quick digital scan of your teeth instead of messy impression putty and have your guard made from durable material to match your bite. A better fit means a guard you will actually wear.

Guards for athletes

If you or your kids play contact sports, a custom sports mouthguard protects teeth from injury on the field or court. It is a different appliance from a night guard, and we can make either one from the same quick digital scan.

Questionsabout night guards.

How do I know if I need a night guard?

Waking up with a sore jaw or headaches, sensitive teeth, and teeth that look worn or chipped are common signs. We can look for grinding wear at your next visit and tell you if a guard would help.

Is a night guard the same as a sports mouthguard?

No. A sports guard protects your teeth from injury during activity. A night guard protects them from the damage caused by grinding and clenching.

How is a custom guard made?

We take a digital scan of your teeth, which is faster and cleaner than impression putty, and use it to make a guard that fits your bite.

Will a night guard help my headaches or TMJ?

Grinding and clenching often cause tension headaches and jaw pain. A guard relieves pressure on the jaw muscles and is commonly recommended for TMJ symptoms.

How long does a night guard last?

With good care, a guard can last several years. Heavy grinders may wear through theirs sooner and need a replacement.

How do I clean my guard?

Rinse and brush it daily with cool water and a soft toothbrush, and store it dry in a vented case. Hot water can warp it.
