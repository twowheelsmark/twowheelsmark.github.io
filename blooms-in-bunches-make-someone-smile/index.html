Cheerful Flowers from Our Merrick Flower Shop

Some days just need a little color. Our Make Someone Smile collection is full of sunny mixed bouquets, bright seasonal flowers and happy designs made for encouragement, congratulations or an unexpected pick-me-up. At Blooms in Bunches, formerly Flowers by Voegler, each one is designed by hand at 1171 Merrick Avenue and personally delivered across Merrick, Bellmore, East Meadow and the rest of Nassau County.

Add a personal note at checkout, or call (516) 481-1277 and our designers will help you pick something upbeat.

Make Someone Smile FAQs

What flowers are best for making someone smile?

Bright mixed bouquets, sunflowers, gerbera daisies, yellow roses and cheerful seasonal flowers are all great choices.

When should I send a make-someone-smile bouquet?

Anytime. These flowers are made for encouragement, congratulations, thank yous, friendship, get well wishes or an unexpected pick-me-up.

Can I get same-day delivery?

Yes. Blooms in Bunches offers same-day flower delivery across Merrick and Nassau County when you order by our daily cutoff. To confirm timing for a specific address, call us at (516) 481-1277.

Can I send flowers to someone far away?

Yes. Order online with a Nassau County address and we handle everything locally. For recipients farther away, we offer nationwide delivery through our trusted florist network.

What should I write on the card?

Keep it short and personal. Something like "Thinking of you and smiling," "You've got this" or "Just because you're you" works for almost anyone.

Will my flowers arrive in a box?

No. Every arrangement is designed by hand in our Merrick shop and personally delivered by our local team, never shipped in a cardboard box.

Need help choosing?

Call our designers at (516) 481-1277 and we'll help you send a smile.
