Sympathy Flower Delivery FAQs

What is the difference between sympathy flowers and funeral flowers?

Sympathy flowers are usually sent to the family home as a personal gesture of comfort. Funeral flowers, like standing sprays and wreaths, are delivered to the funeral home or church for the service, and casket sprays are usually selected by the immediate family. Shop For the Home or For the Service.

Which funeral homes do you deliver to?

We deliver funeral flowers daily to Monroe County funeral homes, including all four Merkle Funeral Service locations, Bacarella, Rupp, Bobcean in Carleton and Flat Rock, Capaul in Ida, Martenson, Michigan Memorial, Pawlak and Urbanski Bedford Chapel. Call (734) 242-6565 for other locations.

Can I get same-day sympathy flower delivery?

Yes, same-day delivery is available in Monroe and nearby towns. Order before our daily cutoff, and if the service is soon, call us at (734) 242-6565 so we can coordinate timing with you directly.

What should I include for a funeral delivery?

Include the name of the deceased, the funeral home or church, and the date and time of the visitation or service so the flowers arrive before it begins.

What should I write on a sympathy card?

Short and sincere is best. "With deepest sympathy," "Thinking of you and your family" or "Holding you close in our thoughts" are always appropriate. Add a fond memory if you have one, and sign your full name so the family knows who sent the flowers.

Do you offer lasting memorial gifts?

Yes. Alongside fresh flowers, we offer memorial keepsakes including memory stones, wind chimes, cotton throws and quilts, lanterns, and memorial benches and birdbaths. See our Keepsakes.
