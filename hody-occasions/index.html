Flowers for Every Occasion, Delivered Across Nashville

Whatever you are celebrating or marking, Hody's has been helping Nashville say it with flowers since 1950. Every arrangement is designed by hand in our own shop and delivered by our own drivers to homes, offices, hospitals, and funeral homes throughout Nashville, Franklin, Brentwood, and the surrounding Middle Tennessee communities.

Not sure what to choose? Give our designers a budget and a color palette and let them create something with the freshest flowers of the day, or call the shop and we will help you pick.

Why Nashville Chooses Hody's

Family-Owned Since 1950

THREE GENERATIONS OF NASHVILLE FLORISTS

Robbie Hody opened the shop in 1950, and Hody's is now in its third generation. For more than 70 years, Nashville families have trusted us with the flowers that mark their most important days.

Hand-Arranged

DESIGNED IN-HOUSE, NEVER SHIPPED IN A BOX

Every arrangement is handcrafted by our florists and hand-delivered, never shipped in a cardboard box. Some of our designs are proprietary to Hody's, so you will not find them anywhere else.

Same-Day Delivery

OUR OWN FLEET OF FIVE DELIVERY VANS

Our service area is divided into five sections, with a dedicated van for each one, so your flowers arrive promptly. We even send a real-time email confirmation when your delivery has been made.

Flower Delivery FAQs

Can I get same-day flower delivery in Nashville?

Yes. Hody's makes same-day deliveries in Nashville, Franklin, Brentwood, Cool Springs, Antioch, Bellevue, Goodlettsville, Hermitage, Hendersonville, Joelton, and Madison. Order early in the day for the best selection, or call us at (615) 876-7181 for anything time-sensitive.

I do not know which flowers to choose. Can you pick for me?

Yes. Give our florists a budget and a color palette, and our designers will create something with the freshest stems that came in that morning. Or call (615) 876-7181 and we will help you choose.

Can you deliver to an office, hospital, or funeral home?

Yes. Our drivers deliver to homes, offices, Nashville hospitals, senior living communities, and funeral homes every day. Include the recipient's full name and any room, department, or service details in your order.

Can I request a morning delivery?

We honor AM delivery requests in most cases, with a premium service charge added for the expedited delivery. AM delivery is not available on holidays.

Do you deliver outside the Nashville area?

Yes. For deliveries outside our area, we send your order through a network of preferred Teleflora florists we have built over more than 70 years, and every wired order is backed by our satisfaction guarantee.

What if I am not happy with my arrangement?

Every arrangement is backed by our satisfaction guarantee. If your flowers do not meet your expectations, contact us and we will replace them promptly. Replacements are honored within 72 hours of delivery.

Need Help Choosing?

Call our Nashville shop at (615) 876-7181 or our Cool Springs shop at (615) 373-9887 and our designers will help you find the perfect flowers for any occasion.
