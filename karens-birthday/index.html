Birthday Flower Delivery FAQs

Can I get same-day birthday flower delivery?

Yes, same-day delivery is available. Order earlier in the day for the best chance of a same-day run, and if your delivery is time sensitive call us at (905) 878-2881 so we can confirm the schedule before you order. We also deliver on Sundays to select areas.

What are the best flowers for a birthday?

Bright mixed bouquets with gerbera daisies, roses, lilies and sunflowers are the most popular choices. Roses in their favourite colour, a blooming plant or their birth month flower make it more personal. If you are unsure, our designers are happy to suggest something.

Can I add balloons or a gift to birthday flowers?

Yes. Add a Balloon Bouquet or pair your flowers with something from our Gift Sets collection to round out the celebration.

Can I include a personal card message?

Of course. Every order comes with a card, and we write your message exactly as you enter it at checkout. If you are ordering by phone, our team will read it back to you before the order is placed.

Do you deliver birthday flowers across Toronto and the GTA?

Yes. We deliver birthday flowers throughout Toronto, including North York, Etobicoke and Scarborough, and across the GTA to Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Oakville, Burlington and more. If you are not sure we reach an address, call us at (905) 878-2881 and we will let you know.

How do I keep birthday flowers fresh?

Trim the stems, top up or change the water every day or two, and keep the arrangement away from direct sun and heat. Our flower care guide has more tips to help your flowers last.

Need Help Choosing?
