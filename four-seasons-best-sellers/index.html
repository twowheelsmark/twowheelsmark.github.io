About Four Seasons Flowers

7

Four Seasons Flowers began in September 1974, when Barbara Bertran opened a family plant shop in Poway. Her daughter Carma and Carma’s husband David run the business today from our shop in the Vons center on Black Mountain Road in Rancho Peñasquitos.

The bouquets on this page are the ones our designers make most often, so they get plenty of practice. You get the same polished look whether you order online, call the shop, or walk in and pick one up.

Best Seller Questions

10
What makes a bouquet one of your best sellers?

These are simply the designs our customers order most. We update the collection as the seasons change, so the colors and flowers shift through the year with whatever is freshest from our growers.

Can I get a best seller delivered today?

Yes. Same-day delivery is available, and the earlier in the day you order, the better. On busy days our delivery schedule can fill up, so if timing matters, order early or call us at 858.484.7700.

Where do you deliver in San Diego?

We deliver throughout North County San Diego, as far south as downtown, east to Ramona and Tierrasanta, and west to the beach communities. The delivery charge is based on the recipient’s zip code. If your recipient lives farther away, we can send your order through a trusted florist in their area.

Will my bouquet look exactly like the photo?

We design each bouquet to match the photo as closely as we can. Now and then a flower is out of season or unavailable, and we swap in something similar while keeping the same color theme, quality and value.

Can I order online and pick it up at the shop?

Yes. Choose pick up when you order on our website and select the time you would like it ready. Our shop is at 13289 Black Mountain Road in the Vons center in Rancho Peñasquitos.

Can I add chocolates, a balloon or a gift?

Yes. Browse our chocolates, balloons and gift collection and add a little something extra to the same order.
