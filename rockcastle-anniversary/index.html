Anniversary Flowers & Gifts

Send Your Spouse Beautiful Fresh Flowers

Rockcastle Florist offers a great selection of Anniversary Flowers, Plants and Gifts. May we suggest a few options for you? How about a dozen, gorgeous Ecuadorian Roses. We buy farm-direct so our Ecuadorian Roses have longer stems, larger blooms and provide extended vase life. Nothing says I love you quite like a dozen beautiful roses and a box of gourmet Godiva chocolates. Looking for something other than roses. How about a luxurious and unique, custom floral arrangement. Our design team creates some of the most talked about arrangements in the Rochester and Canandaigua NY area.

Due to various seasonal and regional conditions, and the variety of unplanned issues courtesy of COVID 19 and Mother Nature, availability of some flowers may be very limited in certain areas. Also, specific flower varieties or colors may not be available for delivery on a specified day. Therefore, it is possible that the exact bouquet pictured or requested may not be available for delivery. Occasionally, substitutions may be necessary to create your bouquet due to the availability of certain flowers in various parts of the country. Care is taken to maintain the style, theme and color scheme of the arrangement, using flowers of equal value. Additionally, the substitution of certain keepsake items may be necessary due to increased demand, especially during major holidays. In single-flower arrangements, such as an all rose bouquet, or orchids, we will make every attempt to match the flower type, but may substitute with another color. In this event, the local florist will deliver a second choice of similar flowers and container of equal value and beauty. Every effort will be made to maintain size, shape, and overall color schemes of an arrangement, but there may be some variance due to designer interpretation. In single variety arrangements, variety will take preference over color. Our goal is your complete satisfaction, rest assured that every effort will be made to provide an arrangement of equal or greater value.
