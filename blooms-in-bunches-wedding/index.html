Wedding and Event Flower FAQs

Does Blooms in Bunches do wedding flowers?

Yes. We design bridal bouquets, wedding ceremony flowers and centerpieces for weddings across Merrick and Long Island. Explore our gallery, then request a consultation to plan your florals with our designers.

How do I book a wedding flower consultation?

Use our wedding consultation form or call (516) 481-1277. We will listen to your ideas and personal style and translate them into the florals for your ceremony, bouquets and reception.

What wedding flowers are in season?

Spring weddings shine with peonies, ranunculus, sweet peas and lilacs. Summer suits dahlias, sunflowers and anthurium. Autumn calls for amaranthus, Black Baccara roses and proteas, and winter favors white amaryllis, hellebores and anemones.

Do you do flowers for other events?

Yes. We specialize in corporate and personal events, including sweet 16s, bar and bat mitzvahs, showers and parties, and we are known for our attention to detail.

Do you make corsages and boutonnieres?

Yes. We make corsages and boutonnieres for weddings, proms and other events. Call (516) 481-1277 to order.

Do you offer party rentals?

Yes. Along with flowers, our shop offers party rentals. Call (516) 481-1277 to ask what is available for your date.
