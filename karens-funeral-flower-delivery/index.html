Timing, Etiquette and What to Know

  • Plan for delivery the day before or the morning of the service. That gives the funeral home time to display the arrangements before family and guests arrive. Most funeral homes accept deliveries between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM.
  • Put the name of the person who has died in the recipient field. Add the funeral home or church name and address, the service date and time, and your card message. Funeral homes look after several families at once and need the name to place your tribute.
  • The casket spray comes from the immediate family. Friends, neighbours and coworkers usually send a standing spray, a wreath or an arrangement for the family's home.
  • Sign the card the way the family knows you. A first and last name, or a company name with the department the family knows, is more helpful than a surname alone.
  • Keep the card message simple. "Thinking of you and your family during this difficult time." "With deepest sympathy and love." "In loving memory of someone so special."
  • Sympathy flowers can be sent at any time. Many people send flowers to the service or the visitation, and others send an arrangement to the family's home in the days or weeks afterward.
  • Service later today or first thing tomorrow? Call (905) 878-2881 rather than ordering online and we will tell you straight away what we can do.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should funeral flowers be delivered?

The day before or the morning of the service, so the arrangements can be displayed before family members and guests arrive. Tell us the earlier of the visitation and service times when you order.

What information do you need to send flowers to a funeral home?

The full name of the person who has died (in the recipient field), the funeral home or church name and address, the date and time of the service, and your card message.

What is the difference between sympathy and funeral flowers?

Funeral flowers are delivered to the funeral home, church or memorial service. Sympathy flowers are usually sent to the family's home or workplace. Both are a way of showing support and remembrance.

Who usually sends the casket spray?

The immediate family, traditionally. Everyone else sends a standing spray, a wreath, a basket or a vase arrangement, or flowers to the family's home.

Which funeral homes do you deliver to?

Funeral homes across Milton, Halton Hills, Oakville, Burlington, Mississauga, Brampton and beyond, including the ones listed above. If yours is not listed, call (905) 878-2881.

Can I send funeral flowers from outside Ontario?

Yes. Order online at karensflowershop.ca or call the shop, and our team will coordinate the delivery with the funeral home. Through our FTD, Teleflora and FSN partnerships we can also send sympathy flowers across Canada, the USA and internationally.
