Hospital Flower Delivery in Milton, Halton and the GTA

Karen's Flower Shop has been sending get well flowers to hospital rooms since 1966. Every arrangement is created by our in-house designers at our family store at 487 Laurier Avenue in Milton and delivered from our location to the hospital, ready for a bedside table.

We deliver to Milton District Hospital, Oakville Trafalgar Memorial, Georgetown Hospital, Joseph Brant in Burlington, the Trillium Health Partners and William Osler hospitals in Mississauga and Brampton, and to long-term care and retirement homes across Halton and Peel. Same-day delivery is available; order earlier in the day for the best chance of a same-day run.

How Hospital Delivery Works

1

Choose an arrangement from our get well collection, or call the shop and tell us about the patient. A designer will suggest something that suits the room and the reason for the stay.

2

Give us the patient's full name, the hospital, and the room or unit number if you have it. Add your own phone number in case anything changes before delivery.

3

We deliver to the hospital. Depending on the unit, the arrangement goes to the room, the nursing station or the front desk, following that hospital's rules.

Before You Order

  • We need the patient's full name and the hospital. A room or unit number gets the flowers there faster. Hospital staff cannot look up a patient from a first name alone.
  • Some units do not allow fresh flowers or plants. Intensive care, critical care, neonatal intensive care, oncology and transplant units commonly restrict them, and policies change. Call the unit before you order, or call us at (905) 878-2881 and we will help you check.
  • Keep the scent light. Halton Healthcare, which runs Milton District, Oakville Trafalgar and Georgetown hospitals, asks visitors not to bring highly fragrant flowers such as lilies and hyacinths. Joseph Brant Hospital in Burlington does not allow them anywhere in the hospital, and does not allow cut flowers or plants in its ICU. Our designers keep hospital arrangements low in scent.
  • Latex balloons are not allowed at Halton Healthcare, Joseph Brant or William Osler hospitals. If you would like to add a balloon to a hospital order, call us first.
  • If the patient is moved or discharged, call us as soon as you know so we can update the delivery address before the order goes out.
  • Ordering from out of town? Many of our hospital orders come from across Canada and overseas. Order online at karensflowershop.ca or call the shop and we will look after the details.
  • Sunday delivery is available to select areas, and the shop is open Sunday from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM. Call to confirm a Sunday hospital delivery before you order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you deliver flowers to a hospital today?

Yes, same-day delivery is available. Order earlier in the day for the best chance of a same-day run, and if the delivery is time sensitive call us at (905) 878-2881 so we can confirm the schedule before you order.

What information do you need to send flowers to a patient?

The patient's full name, the hospital, and the room or unit number if you have it. Your own phone number helps too, in case the patient has been moved and we need to reach you.

Are flowers allowed in the ICU?

Usually not. Intensive care, critical care and neonatal intensive care units generally do not allow fresh flowers or plants. Joseph Brant Hospital, for example, does not allow cut flowers or plants in its ICU. Call the unit first, or call us and we will help. Flowers can often be sent to the family at home instead.

Do you deliver to Milton District Hospital?

Yes. Milton District Hospital on Bronte Street South is a few minutes from our shop on Laurier Avenue, and we deliver there regularly, along with Oakville Trafalgar Memorial and Georgetown Hospital.

Do you deliver to long-term care and retirement homes?

Yes. We deliver to long-term care, retirement and assisted living homes across Milton, Oakville, Burlington and Halton Hills. Include the resident's full name and room number, and we will handle the front desk.

Can I order from outside Ontario for a patient here?

Yes, and many of our hospital orders come from out of province or overseas. Order online at karensflowershop.ca or call (905) 878-2881 and speak with our team.
