Autumn Color from Our Bedford Village Studio

As summer comes to a close, autumn foliage and flowers make their way into our shop. Creating fall arrangements in the reds, oranges and golds of the season is our specialty, from rich autumn blooms and vibrant mums to dahlias and warm fall textures.

Hosting this season? Our designers create centerpieces for the Thanksgiving table and arrangements for the entry or sideboard, and a fall bouquet makes a thoughtful hostess gift for the friends who invite you over.

Why Bedford Village Flower Shoppe for Fall Flowers?

Reason #1

World-Class Designers

Every fall arrangement starts with our owner and lead designer, Lauren Chillemi, EMC, one of the few Master European Floral Designers in the world.

Lauren personally trains every designer in our shop through a rigorous 4-level curriculum, so the centerpiece you send is built with the same eye for color, balance and texture she brings to her own work.

Reason #2

Fresh, Seasonal Flowers

Seasonal flowers are fresher, last longer and carry all the colors of autumn. Lauren oversees all of our flower purchasing and has built strong relationships with wholesalers, distributors and local farms.

Depending on the design, our blooms come from as far as Italy or as close as upstate New York and Westchester County.

Reason #3

Customer-Obsessed

Owners Lauren and Anthony Chillemi live just down the road from the shop, are in the shop all week, and personally handle any question about your order.

Every arrangement is backed by our 96-hour guarantee. If you are not happy with your arrangement, we will gladly provide a full refund, no questions asked.

Reason #4

Designed to Last

How an arrangement is built matters as much as the flowers in it. Some beautiful blooms last longer in designs that follow their natural shape instead of floral foam or chicken wire.

Our fall collections are built to stay beautiful through the holiday weekend, not just for one dinner.

In Season This Fall

These are the flowers and textures that come into their own as the leaves turn. Ask for a favorite when you order, or call the shop and we will build an arrangement around it.

Dahlias

Late season favorites with layered petals in burgundy, coral and gold. See our Dahlias for Days collection.

Mums

Full, long lasting blooms in bronze, rust and cream that give fall arrangements their classic look.

Roses and Hydrangeas

Garden roses in terracotta and blush, and antique hydrangeas that deepen in color as the season goes on.

Fall Textures

Berries, branches and warm foliage add depth and make an arrangement feel like autumn.

Planning a Thanksgiving table? Call us at 914-234-7180 to talk through the size of your table and the colors you love, and order your centerpiece ahead of the holiday.

Fall Flower FAQs

What flowers are in season in the fall?

Dahlias, mums, garden roses and hydrangeas in warm autumn tones are at their best in the fall. Our designers pair them with berries, branches and seasonal foliage for a true fall look.

Do you make Thanksgiving centerpieces?

Yes. Our designers create centerpieces for the Thanksgiving table as well as arrangements for the entry, sideboard or kitchen island. Call 914-234-7180 to talk through sizes and colors for your table.

Can I get same-day delivery on fall flowers?

Yes. Order online by 3pm Monday through Friday, or by noon on Saturday, for same-day delivery in Bedford and the surrounding towns. Past the cutoff? Call us at 914-234-7180 and we will check what we can do.

How do I keep my fall arrangement fresh?

Re-cut the tips of the stems and change the water daily, and keep the arrangement away from drafty windows and doors and from heating vents. See our tips for caring for fresh flowers.

Do you deliver fall flowers to Connecticut?

Yes. Along with northern Westchester, we deliver to Southern Fairfield County, including Greenwich, Cos Cob, Stamford, New Canaan and Ridgefield.

What if I am not happy with my fall arrangement?

Every arrangement is backed by our 96-hour guarantee. If you are not happy with your arrangement, we will gladly provide a full refund, no questions asked.
