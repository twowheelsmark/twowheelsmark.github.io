Timing and Etiquette

A few things worth knowing before you order.

⏱

Aim for the viewing

If there is a viewing before the funeral, have your flowers there for it. That is when the family sees them longest.

✎

Keep the card simple

Your name and a few sincere words are enough. For a group gift, sign with the group's name.

♡

Follow the family's wishes

If the obituary asks for donations in lieu of flowers, a donation or a modest piece sent to the home is the respectful choice.

✦

Know the customs

Flowers are not traditional at Jewish funerals or during shiva. A fruit or food basket sent to the home is a thoughtful alternative.

✿

Think about pets

Lilies are toxic to cats. If the family has a cat, choose a food basket or one of our pet friendly gifts.

☆

It is never too late

Flowers or a plant that arrive a week or two after the service, once the visitors have gone, are often the most appreciated.

Sympathy Flower FAQs

Can't find your answer? Call (610) 272-2233 or text 610.539.5399.

Can I get same-day sympathy flower delivery?
Yes. We deliver sympathy and funeral flowers the same day, seven days a week, including Sundays, across Philadelphia and Bucks, Chester, Delaware and Montgomery Counties. If the service is later today, call (610) 272-2233 so we can confirm the timing with you.
Should I send flowers to the funeral home or the family's home?
Formal pieces like standing sprays, wreaths and photo or urn tributes belong at the funeral home or church. Bouquets, plants and food baskets are better sent to the family's home, where they can be enjoyed after the service. Many people send one of each.
Who usually sends the casket spray?
The casket spray rests on top of the casket and is usually chosen by the immediate family. If you are a friend, coworker or extended family, a standing spray, wreath or service arrangement is the better choice.
What do you need to deliver to a funeral home?
The name of the funeral home or church, the full name of the person who passed, and the date and time of the viewing or service. If you are not sure which funeral home is handling the arrangements, call us and we will help you find out.
What should I write on a sympathy card?
Keep it short and sincere. “With deepest sympathy,” “Thinking of you and your family,” or “Forever in our hearts” are all appropriate. Add a personal memory if you have one, and sign with your full name so the family knows who sent it.
Can you deliver to a funeral outside the Philadelphia area?
Yes. For a service anywhere in the US, we send your tribute through our hand-selected network of partner florists, backed by the same on-time delivery and satisfaction guarantees. Order online or call (610) 272-2233 with the funeral home's name and service times. We can also send flowers internationally.
Can you create a custom funeral piece?
Yes. Our designers can build a tribute around a favorite color, flower, team or hobby. Describe what you have in mind on our Custom Order page, or call us to talk it through.
