3Anniversary Flower Delivery in Los Angeles, CA

Whether it is your first year or your fiftieth, anniversary flowers should feel like you. We have been helping Los Angeles couples say it with flowers since 1970, from a single dozen red roses to arrangements big enough to fill a room.

4

Why Los Angeles Sends Anniversary Flowers From Dottie's

Family Owned Since 1970

Our story started in the late 1960s with a flower stand on a Hollywood street corner. Today the same family still runs the shop, named to honor the grandmother who worked beside our founder from the very beginning, and we treat every anniversary order like it is going to one of our own.

Designed In Our Own Shop

Every arrangement is put together by our floral team in Los Angeles, using as many local and California grown flowers and gifts as we can. Your order never gets handed off to a stranger. It stays with us from the moment you place it until it reaches the door.

Delivered By Our Own Drivers

Our drivers carry your flowers in vans fitted to keep arrangements cool and steady, and you get a confirmation once they are delivered. Need it there for a dinner or a morning surprise? Pick a delivery window at checkout.

5

Anniversary Flowers Delivered All Over Los Angeles

Our flagship shop at 18500 Sherman Way in Reseda sends anniversary flowers across most of north and central Los Angeles, the whole San Fernando Valley and parts of Ventura County. On any given day our drivers might drop roses at a home in Sherman Oaks, an office in Downtown Los Angeles and a restaurant in Santa Monica, and we know the neighborhoods in between, from Encino and Studio City to Burbank and Pasadena.

Most of our anniversary customers send flowers to the house or to work so they arrive before the celebration starts. If you would rather surprise them in person, you can pick up your order at our Reseda shop. Planning further ahead for a big milestone? You can schedule delivery for any future date online or by phone, and ordering early gives you the best choice of roses.

Sending from out of state or overseas? Plenty of our customers do. Place your order online, add your card message and we will take care of the rest right here in Los Angeles.

7Anniversary Flower Questions

Can you deliver anniversary flowers today in Los Angeles? +

Yes. We offer same-day anniversary flower delivery across our local area when you order early in the day. If it is getting late, call our shop at (818) 996-2603 and we will tell you honestly what we can still do.

What are the best flowers for an anniversary? +

Red roses are the classic choice because they say romance without any explanation. For a milestone year, a two dozen or 100 rose arrangement makes a real statement. If your partner loves softer colors, a garden style bouquet, lilies or orchids feel just as personal. Our rose color guide can help you choose.

Can I choose a delivery time for an anniversary surprise? +

Yes. At checkout you can choose AM Priority, PM Priority or Express Delivery for a set window, which is perfect when the flowers need to arrive before dinner or while your partner is at home. Standard deliveries arrive during business hours.

Can you deliver anniversary flowers to an office? +

Absolutely. We deliver to offices, hotels and other businesses all over Los Angeles. Add the suite number and the recipient's phone number so our driver can find them quickly.

Do anniversary flowers come with a card? +

Every order includes a free enclosure card with your personal message. If you are stuck on what to write, our card message ideas have plenty of anniversary wording to start from.

Can you make a custom anniversary arrangement? +

Yes. Tell us her favorite flowers, the colors from your wedding or the number of years you are celebrating, and our designers will build something just for the two of you. Call us at (818) 996-2603 to talk it through.
