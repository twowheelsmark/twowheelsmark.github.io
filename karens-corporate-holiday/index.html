Corporate Order Questions

How early should we place our holiday order?

As early as possible. Pre-ordering secures your delivery dates before the December rush and gives you the widest choice of designs. We're happy to start planning now and finalize details closer to your date.

Can you deliver gifts to a list of different addresses?

Yes. Send us your recipient list with names, addresses and card messages, and we'll take care of every delivery across Toronto and the GTA.

Can you match our company colours or event theme?

Absolutely. Our designers can create centrepieces and arrangements in your brand colours or to suit your venue and theme.

Do you only offer what's shown on this page?

Not at all. These are a few favourites from our holiday collection. Tell us your budget and what you have in mind, and we'll recommend options or design something custom.
