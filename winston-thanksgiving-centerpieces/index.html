Thanksgiving Centerpieces for a Beautiful Holiday Table

The best meal of the year deserves nothing less than a beautifully dressed table. Our Thanksgiving Centerpiece collection features the most spectacular flora of the autumn season, including glowing blooms in natural hues and fresh fall foliage for organic texture. Each of our Thanksgiving flower centerpieces is artfully designed and arranged with the utmost care—because we believe that your holiday table should be as beautiful as the memories made around it.

Choose a single centerpiece for the middle of the table or a trio of smaller arrangements to run its length. Each one is made by our Boston designers and delivered throughout Greater Boston, so your table is ready before the first guest arrives. Celebrating at someone else's home this year? A centerpiece makes a warm thank-you for the host. For more seasonal designs, explore our Autumn Collection.

Why Choose Winston Flowers

80+ Years of Boston Holiday Tables

Founded in 1944 by Robert Winston, Winston Flowers is a third-generation, family-owned business run by brothers David and Ted Winston. For over eight decades, our flowers have been part of Boston's holiday gatherings.

Centerpieces Designed for the Table

Our designers create Thanksgiving centerpieces and trios with seasonal blooms and fall foliage, and our collections are refreshed 9 times per year, so your table always feels fresh, seasonal and inspired.

Same-Day Delivery & Concierge Service

We source the highest quality flowers, including rare imports most florists can't access, and offer same-day delivery throughout Greater Boston. Every order is handled with 5-star concierge care, from design to your doorstep.

A Host Gift That Arrives Ready

Every Winston Flowers centerpiece arrives in our signature luxury packaging, ready to set on the table. Add a handwritten note to send your thanks to whoever is hosting this year.

Award-Winning Excellence

Winston Flowers has been recognized as “Florist of the Year” and named Boston’s “Best Of” year after year. When you want your holiday table to look its very best, you will find it here.

Thanksgiving week is one of the busiest times of the year for our designers, so we recommend ordering a few days ahead for the best selection and your preferred delivery date. Same-day delivery is available throughout Greater Boston on orders placed by 1:00 PM EST, but holiday delivery times fill quickly, so ordering early guarantees your spot.

A centerpiece that sits low lets guests see and talk across the table. Call us at (800) 457-4901 and our team will help you choose.

Keep your centerpiece in a cool spot away from heating vents, fireplaces and direct sun, and check the water level daily, topping it up with fresh, cool water as needed. If it arrives a day or two before the holiday, keep it in the coolest room of the house until it is time to set the table. Removing any blooms as they fade keeps the arrangement looking its best.
