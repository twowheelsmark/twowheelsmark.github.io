Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most about ordering, same-day delivery in Dallas, funeral and hospital deliveries, weddings and our family shop on Garland Road. Can't find your answer? Call us at (214) 324-2481.

2Ordering

How do I order flowers from McShan Florist?

Order online at mcshanflorist.com, call our Dallas shop at (214) 324-2481 or toll free at 1-800-MCSHANS, or stop by 10311 Garland Road. Every arrangement is designed by hand at the shop. You can browse best sellers, shop by occasion, or start with customer favorites.

What happens if a flower in my arrangement is not available?

Flowers are seasonal, so now and then we substitute to keep your arrangement fresh. We use flowers of equal or greater value and keep the shape, size, style and colors of the design.

In a single-variety arrangement the flower comes first and the color second, so a yellow daisy order becomes daisies in another color rather than a different yellow flower. White and cream roses can stand in for each other, as can peach and pink roses. Our full substitution policy covers plants, gift baskets and balloons too.

Is my order guaranteed?

Yes. Every order is backed by our promise of 100% satisfaction or your money back. If something is not right, call us at (214) 324-2481 and we will make it right. Read our promise.

Can I send flowers internationally?

Yes, but international orders have to be placed by phone. We cannot accept them online. Call (214) 324-2481 and we will arrange delivery through our network of florists overseas.

Do you have a rewards program?

Yes. McShan Leaf Rewards gives you 1 point for every dollar you spend. When your balance reaches 250 points we send you a $15 Leaf Certificate to use on your next purchase (gift cards excluded). It is not a charge account, and you can pay however you like. See Leaf Rewards for details.

Do you offer a flower subscription?

Yes. Our monthly subscription plan sends fresh flowers on a regular schedule, for yourself, an office reception desk or someone you want to remember every month.

3Local Delivery

Where does McShan Florist deliver?

We deliver throughout Dallas and the surrounding area, including Addison, Allen, Balch Springs, Carrollton, Cedar Hill, Coppell, DeSoto, Duncanville, Flower Mound, Forney, Frisco, Garland, Grand Prairie, Hutchins, Irving, Lancaster, Lewisville, McKinney, Mesquite, Plano, Red Oak, Richardson, Rockwall, Rowlett, Sachse, Seagoville, Sunnyvale, The Colony, Wilmer and Wylie. See delivery information, or start from our Dallas florist page.

Do you offer same-day flower delivery in Dallas?

Yes. Same-day delivery is available Monday through Saturday. Order early in the day for the best chance of same-day delivery, and if you are ordering later in the afternoon, call (214) 324-2481 and we will tell you what is still possible. Shop same-day flower delivery.

How much is delivery?

Local delivery is $19.95 per address. Deliveries outside our local delivery area may cost more, and the exact fee is shown before you check out.

Do you deliver on Sundays?

No. The shop is closed on Sundays and we do not deliver that day. An order requested for Sunday goes out on the next business day.

Can I choose a specific delivery time?

We will do our best to deliver at the time of day you ask for, but we cannot guarantee an exact time. If timing matters, for a surprise or an event, call (214) 324-2481 and tell us about it.

Do you deliver to Dallas hospitals?

Yes, every day. We deliver to Baylor University Medical Center, Baylor Scott & White Medical Center White Rock, Texas Health Presbyterian Hospital Dallas, Medical City Dallas, Parkland, William P. Clements Jr. University Hospital, Children's Medical Center, Methodist Dallas, Texas Scottish Rite Hospital and the VA Medical Center, among others.

Include the patient's full name and room number if you have it. Some hospitals do not allow latex balloons, so we substitute Mylar balloons when needed. Shop get well flowers.

Do you deliver to nursing homes and senior living communities?

Yes. We deliver daily to senior living and care communities across Dallas, including Presbyterian Village North, The Plaza at Edgemere, Juliette Fowler Homes, Brookdale White Rock, Brookdale Lake Highlands and The Villages of Lake Highlands. If you do not see the community you need, call (214) 324-2481.

How far ahead should I order for a holiday?

Order at least one day ahead for Thanksgiving, Christmas, New Year's Day, Valentine's Day, Easter, Administrative Professionals Week, Mother's Day and the other major holidays. Earlier is better for Valentine's Day and Mother's Day.

4Sympathy & Funeral

Do you deliver to funeral homes in Dallas?

Yes, every day. We deliver to funeral homes across Dallas, including Restland Funeral Home, Laurel Land, Sparkman/Hillcrest, Calvary Hill, Grove Hill, Hughes Family Tribute Center, Golden Gate, Black & Clark, Lincoln Funeral Home and North Dallas Funeral Home.

Give us the full name of the person who has died, the funeral home and the date and time of the service. If you do not see the funeral home you need, call (214) 324-2481 and we will work it out.

What should I send to the service, and what should go to the home?

For the service, families and close friends usually choose casket sprays and standing sprays and wreaths, which are displayed at the funeral home or church. Browse everything for the service.

For the family's home, a vase arrangement or a sympathy plant is more practical, and a plant lasts long after the service. See flowers for the home.

Who sends the casket spray?

Traditionally the immediate family: a spouse, children or parents. Friends, coworkers and neighbors usually send a standing spray, a wreath or an arrangement for the family's home instead. If you are not sure, call us and a designer will help you choose.

Can you deliver flowers to a cemetery?

We cannot guarantee deliveries to cemeteries or rural route addresses. For a graveside service, we recommend sending the flowers to the funeral home, which can take them to the cemetery with the family.

Do you have flowers for the loss of a pet?

Yes. Our pet sympathy collection is a gentle way to let someone know you understand what they have lost.

Do you handle corporate and private events?

Yes. We design flowers for conventions, business seminars, corporate celebrations and retirement parties, and for private events such as anniversaries, birthdays, baptisms, bar mitzvahs, christenings, Sweet 16s, quinceañeras and dinners. See our wedding and event services.

Can you decorate a home or office for the holidays?

Yes. For every holiday through the year we design custom residential and corporate decor, with on-site staging, installation and take down. Our staff will make recommendations based on your vision, your space and your budget.

Do you make corsages and boutonnieres?

Yes. Order corsages and boutonnieres online for proms, homecomings, weddings and dances.

7Flower Care

How do I make my flowers last longer?

Top up the vase with fresh water every day and change it completely every two or three days. Trim about an inch from each stem at an angle when you change the water, and pull any leaves that sit below the waterline. Keep the arrangement out of direct sun and away from heating vents and fruit bowls.

How do I care for a plant from McShan Florist?

Most of our plants like bright, indirect light and water only when the top of the soil feels dry. Too much water is a more common problem than too little. If a plant came with a care card, follow it.

Are any flowers dangerous for pets?

Yes. Lilies are highly toxic to cats, including the pollen and the vase water. If the recipient has a cat, tell us and we will design without lilies.

8About the Shop

How long has McShan Florist been in business?

Who runs McShan Florist?

Bruce A. McShan, President, joined the family business full time after graduating from Stephen F. Austin State University in 1970. He is a lifetime member of the Texas State Florists' Association and an FTD Lifetime Member Achievement Award recipient.

His daughter Jodi McShan, Vice President, is the third generation. She is certified by the American Institute of Floral Designers (AIFD), Professional Floral Communicators International (PFCI) and the American Academy of Floriculture (AAF), and is a Texas Master Florist.

Has McShan Florist won any awards?
