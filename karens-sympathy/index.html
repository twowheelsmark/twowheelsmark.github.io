Sympathy Flower Delivery FAQs

What is the difference between sympathy flowers and funeral flowers?

Sympathy flowers are usually sent to the family home or workplace as a personal gesture of support. Funeral flowers, like standing sprays, wreaths and casket sprays, are delivered to the funeral home or church for the service. Shop Home or Office or Funeral arrangements.

Can you deliver to a funeral home or church?

Yes. Include the name of the deceased, the funeral home or church, and the date and time of the visitation or service in your order so the flowers arrive before it begins. If the service is soon, call us at (905) 878-2881 to confirm timing before you order.

Can I get same-day sympathy flower delivery?

Yes, same-day delivery is available. Order earlier in the day for the best chance of a same-day run, and call us at (905) 878-2881 if the timing is important so we can confirm the schedule.

What should I write on a sympathy card?

Short and sincere is best. Messages like "With deepest sympathy," "Thinking of you and your family," or "Holding you close in our thoughts" are always appropriate. Add a fond memory if you have one, and sign with your full name so the family knows who sent the flowers.

Do you deliver sympathy flowers across Toronto and the GTA?

Yes. We deliver to homes, funeral homes and churches throughout Toronto, including North York, Etobicoke and Scarborough, and across the GTA to Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Oakville, Burlington and more. If you are not sure we reach an address, call us and we will let you know.

Can I send sympathy flowers from outside Canada?

Yes. Many of our customers order from overseas or other provinces. Order online or call the shop, and we will make sure your flowers and card message reach the family.
